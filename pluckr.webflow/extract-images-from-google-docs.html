Extract Images from Google Docs

Open the sidebar, see every image in the document, then take one or take them all.

The short version: Pluckr is a Google Docs add-on. It lists every inline image in your Doc, including images inside tables and other tabs, and lets you copy, rename or download them, or save a ZIP of the originals.

Google Docs has no “save all images” command. You can right-click one image at a time, or download the whole Doc as a web page and dig through a folder of files called image1, image2 and so on. Pluckr puts the images in a sidebar next to the Doc instead, so you can see what you have before you take it.

What you see for each image

  • A preview large enough to tell the images apart, in the order they appear in the Doc.
  • A file name such as image-01.png. Click the pencil to rename it.
  • The dimensions and file size, for example 2046 × 1364 and 213.3 KB, so you can spot the heavy ones.
  • Copy and Download buttons. Copy puts the image on your clipboard as a PNG. Download saves that one file.

How to extract the images

  1. Open the Google Doc.
  2. Choose Extensions > Pluckr > Extract images. The sidebar opens on the right.
  3. The first time, Google asks you to allow the add-on. Pluckr asks for access to the current document only, plus your Google account identity so it can check your licence. The privacy policy lists every permission and why.
  4. Unlock Pluckr if you have not already. It is a one-time $5 purchase, and the unlock screen explains the terms before you pay.
  5. Scroll through the list. Rename, copy or download single images, or press Download originals to get all of them in one ZIP named after the Doc.

Google Slides too

Open the same sidebar in a Slides deck and Pluckr lists the pictures on every slide, including ones inside groups, named by slide: slide-01-image-01, slide-01-image-02 and so on. Select, rename, convert and download work the same way, and one purchase covers Docs and Slides. Images used as shape fills or slide backgrounds are not listed.

Alt text

Pluckr shows each image’s alt text and tells you how many images have none. Filter to the ones with no alt text, write a description in the sidebar and save it back to the file, or add alt text files (CSV and JSON) to your download so it travels with the images.

Images in tables and other tabs

Images placed inside table cells are listed with the rest, and so are images on other document tabs. You do not have to open each tab and scan it separately.

After you edit the Doc

The list shows the Doc as it was when Pluckr last scanned it. Add, remove or replace images, then press the refresh icon at the top of the sidebar to scan again.

The originals, untouched

Download originals gives you the images as they are stored in the Doc, with no recompression. If you want smaller files, open Output settings and convert them. That is covered on the page about converting Google Docs images to WebP or AVIF.

What it does not cover yet

Pluckr reads inline images. Images set to float in front of or behind text are not listed yet, and neither are drawings. If an image you expect is missing, check its wrap setting in Docs.

Price and privacy

Pluckr costs $5 once. There is no subscription, and the terms include a 14-day refund. Your images are read by Google Apps Script inside your Google account and handled in your browser. They are not sent to Pluckr’s servers. What Pluckr does store is a licence record: your Google account ID, your email and the purchase details.

Questions

Does Pluckr change my Google Doc?
No. It reads the images and shows them in the sidebar. Your document stays as it is.

Does it find images inside tables?
Yes. Images in tables and in other document tabs are listed with the rest.

Do I have to publish the Doc?
No. Pluckr works on the Doc you have open. There is nothing to publish or share.

Are the downloaded images the same quality as in the Doc?
Yes. Download originals gives you the stored images without recompressing them. Quality only changes if you choose to convert.

Can I extract images from a Doc someone shared with me?
You need to be able to open the Doc and use add-ons in it. Pluckr reads the Doc you have open, so access follows your permissions in Google Docs.

Does it work in Google Slides?
Yes. Open Pluckr from a Slides deck to list, rename, convert and download its pictures. Markdown export is only for Docs.

What file names do I get?
image-01, image-02 and so on, in document order. You can rename any of them before you download.
