Convert Google Docs Images to WebP or AVIF

Pick a format, set the quality, and check the new file sizes before you download anything.

The short version: Pluckr converts the images in your Google Doc to AVIF, WebP, JPEG or PNG inside the sidebar. You choose quality, a maximum width and an aspect ratio, and the conversion runs in your browser.

Why WebP and AVIF

Both formats make much smaller files than the PNG or JPEG you pasted into the Doc, so pages load faster and uploads go through. In our demo document a 2.5 MB photo came out at about 98 KB as WebP. Your results depend on the image and the quality you pick.

The output settings

  • Format. AVIF, WebP, JPEG or PNG.
  • Quality. A slider for the lossy formats. It starts at 80%.
  • Max width. Set a width in pixels. Images are never enlarged.
  • Aspect ratio. Keep the original, or crop from the centre to 1:1, 4:3, 3:4, 3:2, 2:3, 16:9 or 9:16.

How to convert the images

  1. Open the Doc and choose Extensions > Pluckr > Extract images.
  2. Open Output settings and pick a format, quality, width and aspect ratio.
  3. Convert. Each image shows its new size next to the original.
  4. Download the results.

Which format to pick

  • AVIF gives the smallest files and takes the longest to encode.
  • WebP is the safe all-rounder: small files and wide support.
  • JPEG suits places that still want the oldest format.
  • PNG is lossless. Use it for screenshots and images that need transparency.

Where the conversion runs

In your browser, in the sidebar. The converted files are not uploaded to Pluckr. Your original images in the Doc are not touched. See the privacy policy for the full picture.

Questions

Which formats can Pluckr convert to?
AVIF, WebP, JPEG and PNG.

Are my images uploaded to convert them?
No. Conversion runs in your browser. Google Apps Script reads the images from your Doc, and Pluckr’s servers never receive them.

Can it make every image the same shape?
Yes. Pick an aspect ratio and Pluckr crops each image from the centre.
