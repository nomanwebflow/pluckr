Pluckr is a Google Docs add-on (one-time $5 licence). Test account: pluckr.tester@gmail.com is pre-licensed, so no payment is needed. To test: open the Doc shared with that account (it contains inline images), choose Extensions > Pluckr > Extract images, approve the consent screen, and the sidebar lists the images. The scopes openid and userinfo.email are used only to check the licence: the add-on sends a Google identity token over HTTPS to its licence service (Convex); payments are handled by Polar. Document images and content are never sent to any server. Home page https://docpluckr.com, privacy policy https://docpluckr.com/privacy, terms https://docpluckr.com/terms. Only the four scopes in the manifest are requested. There are no other OAuth clients or projects. Contact: support@docpluckr.com.
